# ACME GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES – EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & COMPANY POLICIES 2026

## 1. Working Hours and Flexible Schedules
ACME Global operates on a standard 40-hour work week, Monday through Friday. All full-time employees observe core business hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time. Employees may arrange flexible start and end times with their direct managers provided that core hours are respected.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Annual Leave
Every full-time team member is entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at a rate of 2.08 days per month. Unused vacation days up to a maximum of 5 days may be rolled over into the subsequent year but must be utilized before March 31st. In addition, ACME provides 10 public holidays and 12 paid sick leave days annually.

## 3. Remote Work & Equipment Stipend
Eligible employees working remotely receive a one-time setup allowance of $1,200 upon hiring to furnish an ergonomic home office. Additionally, employees receive an annual remote work stipend of $600 (disbursed in quarterly increments of $150) to offset high-speed internet and utility expenses.

## 4. Health and Medical Insurance
Health coverage begins on the first day of the calendar month following employment. ACME provides comprehensive medical coverage with an annual inpatient hospitalization limit of $100,000 per dependent. Outpatient prescription drugs are covered at 80% co-insurance up to an annual maximum of $5,000. Vision and dental checks are 100% covered twice per calendar year.

## 5. Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees traveling on approved company business are eligible for expense reimbursements under the following strict limits:
- Daily Meals & Incidentals: Capped at $85 per day for domestic travel and $125 per day for international travel. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable.
- Flights: Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights and any flight segments under 6 hours duration. Business class may be booked only for continuous long-haul international flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.
- Lodging: Preferred corporate hotel rate capped at $220 per night in Tier 2 cities and $320 per night in Tier 1 metropolitan areas.
- Expense Submission: Itemized receipts must be submitted via the finance portal within 30 days of trip conclusion. Claims submitted after 60 days will be forfeited.
